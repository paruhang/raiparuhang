Why these exist

Most Nepali-language utilities online are either abandoned, ad-heavy, or quietly upload your text to a server before "converting" it. This collection exists as a private, fast alternative — built the same way as the rest of raiparuhang.com.np: no accounts, no tracking, and a lookup-table or algorithm doing the actual work locally instead of round-tripping your data. More Nepali-specific tools will land here over time. If there's one you'd find useful, let me know.
