How the fine is calculated

  • 90-day grace period — no fine if you renew within 90 days of your bluebook's expiry date.
  • 5% fine on tax — if you renew within 30 days after the grace period ends.
  • 10% fine — within 45 days after the grace period ends.
  • 20% fine — beyond that, but still within the same fiscal year as expiry.
  • 32% per additional year — once you're renewing in a later fiscal year, up to 5 years.
  • Renewal charge fine (100%) — the flat annual renewal charge itself is doubled once you're past the grace period, separately from the tax fine above.
  • 4-year amnesty — Nepal's Transport Department has an active offer capping payment at 4 years of tax and fines, regardless of how many years are actually overdue.

How to estimate your bluebook renewal fine

Fill in your vehicle details and expiry date, and the estimate updates instantly. Here's the full walkthrough, plus notes on the grace period and amnesty rules.

  1. 1

    Pick your province and vehicle type

    Bagmati's rates are cross-verified against a live renewal service; other provinces are sourced from a single consolidated reference, so treat those as a rougher estimate. Choose two-wheeler, four-wheeler, or the electric equivalent to match your vehicle.

  2. 2

    Enter engine capacity and expiry date

    Type your engine CC (or battery rating for EVs) exactly as printed on your bluebook, then set the expiry date shown there — it's in Bikram Sambat, same as what's on the document.

  3. 3

    Check the box if you already have insurance

    If your third-party insurance is still valid, tick "I already have valid third-party insurance" so it's excluded from the total — otherwise the estimate includes it, since renewal typically requires proof of coverage.

What is the 90-day grace period?

You can renew within 90 days of your bluebook's expiry date with no fine at all — only the base tax and renewal charge apply. The fine only kicks in once you're past that window.

How much is the late fine if I've missed the grace period?

It scales with how overdue you are: 5% within 30 days after the grace period, 10% within 45 days, 20% beyond that but still in the same fiscal year, and 32% per additional year once you're renewing in a later fiscal year. The flat renewal charge itself is also doubled (a separate 100% fine) once you're past the grace period.

What is the 4-year amnesty, and does it apply to me?

Nepal's Transport Department currently caps payment at 4 years of tax and fines, no matter how many years your bluebook has actually been expired. If your calculation shows this applied, the tool flags it directly under your result.

Is this the exact amount I'll pay at the Transport Office?

Treat it as a planning estimate, not a final bill. Provincial governments can change tax rates or amnesty terms with little notice, and only your local Transport Management Office (TMO) can confirm the exact figure. Always verify there before paying, especially for provinces other than Bagmati.

Is my vehicle data uploaded anywhere?

No. The calculation runs entirely in JavaScript in your browser — nothing you enter is sent to a server, stored, or logged.
